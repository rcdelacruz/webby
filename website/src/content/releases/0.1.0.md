The first public build of webby, with installers for all three systems.

- **Tabs.** Open, close and switch; middle-click closes. `window.open` and
  `target=_blank` links open as a tab next to the current one.
- **Address bar** that takes URLs, bare hosts such as `example.com` or
  `localhost:3000`, and search terms (DuckDuckGo).
- **Navigation.** Back, forward, reload, stop, load progress and an HTTPS
  indicator.
- **Start page** listing the sites you visit most.
- **History and session restore.** Tabs reopen where you left off.
- **Light and dark** appearance, following the system.

## Known limits

- Streaming audio and video do not play on Windows.
- No downloads, bookmarks, find in page, history view or private windows.
- No error pages or JavaScript dialogs (`alert`, `confirm`) on macOS and
  Windows.
- The installers are not code signed.
