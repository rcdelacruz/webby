This release is about Windows. macOS and Linux get the new diagnostics
page and are otherwise unchanged from 0.1.0.

## Windows

- **Streaming audio and video.** WebKit's Windows port is built without
  Media Source Extensions and Web Audio, so YouTube and most music and
  video sites could not play in 0.1.0. webby now ships its own build of
  that port, with seventeen patches that add WebKit's GStreamer media
  backend. Checked by hand: YouTube, HLS streams, Web Audio, AAC,
  Dolby Digital Plus 5.1 and H.264 in MP4.
- **GPU video.** Video decoded by Direct3D 11 stays on the GPU from the
  decoder to the screen and no longer passes through the page's main
  thread. Measured on screen in a window: 59.8 frames a second for a
  1080p60 clip and 59.1 for 4K60 VP9.
- **Smooth scrolling.** Wheel and keyboard scrolling are animated, updates
  are paced by the display, and scrolling moves layers instead of
  repainting the page. Screen updates while scrolling went from every
  25 to 80 ms to every 16.7 ms on the pages measured.
- **Display scaling.** Pages drew in the wrong place and ignored clicks at
  125% and 150% scaling. webby is now aware of each monitor's scale.
- **Dark mode.** webby now follows the Windows light or dark setting: the
  title bar, the toolbar, the start page, and what pages are told through
  `prefers-color-scheme`. Until now it was always light on Windows.
- **Fullscreen.** Pages can go fullscreen, which is what a video player's
  fullscreen button asks for. Esc leaves.
- **Site compatibility.** webby identifies itself as Safari and answers
  `mediaCapabilities.decodingInfo()` truthfully, so sites that check
  before offering media no longer turn it away.
- **Fixed** a crash that could follow closing a tab.

## Every platform

- **A darker dark.** The dark appearance of the toolbar and start page is
  now near-black.
- **`webby:media`.** Type it in the address bar to see what this build
  reports to web pages about streaming features and audio and video
  formats.

## Known limits

- Encrypted (DRM) video does not play on Windows.
- On Windows, scrolling a page whose scripts keep it busy, such as YouTube
  while a video plays, is still jerky.
- The installers are still not code signed.
